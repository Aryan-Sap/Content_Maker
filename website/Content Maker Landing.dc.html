Questions,
answered.

Do I need FFmpeg or any other software installed?+

No. The Windows installer bundles FFmpeg, so rendering works out of the box — download, install, drop in a clip.

Is my footage uploaded anywhere?+

Never. Every render happens locally on your machine. The only upload is the one you choose — publishing a finished video to your own YouTube or TikTok account.

How does publishing to YouTube and TikTok work?+

Through each platform's official API. You sign in once in your browser; after that, refresh tokens keep posting hands-off. Instagram Reels opens the file for a quick manual upload.

What platforms does it run on?+

Windows today, as a signed MSIX installer. An Android version with fully native rendering is in active development.

What's the catch? Why free?+

There isn't one. Rendering runs on your hardware, so it costs us nothing to let you make as many Shorts as you want.
